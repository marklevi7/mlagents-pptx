Nora

The assistant who remembers everything.
Nora · your morning briefing

Every morning, before your first coffee, one short message: what happened overnight, who needs a reply today, what’s on your calendar, what’s due this week, who’s waiting on you.

Not a flood of information — a single, distilled signal on one screen. You start the day already on top of it.

Nora · remembers your people

Before every call, Nora reminds you who you’re about to talk to: “This is Anna. Two kids, the younger is four. Last time you discussed the rebrand. You promised to send the quote.”

The kids’ names, the birthdays, what was promised, what was paid, what’s still open — she keeps a living memory of every client, so you walk in prepared. People feel remembered. That turns into loyalty and referrals.

Nora · drafts your replies

Forward Nora a message and she writes the reply in your voice, knowing your whole history with that person.

You glance, tweak if you want, and send it yourself. Nothing leaves without your word. The email that used to cost ten minutes of thinking now costs thirty seconds of editing.

Nora · who, when, why

Nora watches who’s gone quiet, who never got an answer, who’s due an invoice, whose birthday is coming, who promised to call back and vanished.

Not another to-do list you have to remember to check — a live stream of the right nudge at the right moment.

Nora · your memory for money

Nora remembers who pays what and when, which payments are due, which invoices are still open.

Not your accountant — your memory for money. At month’s end, a clean summary instead of chaos, and an answer to “where did it go this month?”

Nora · filters the noise

Email, messengers, newsletters — Nora kills the junk silently and surfaces only what actually needs you.

Your inbox stops being a source of anxiety.

Nora · voice or text, anywhere

Voice or text, in WhatsApp and Telegram. Dictate “remind me to call Katya about the quote tomorrow” from behind the wheel — Nora transcribes it, understands it, and handles it.
